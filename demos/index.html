Where to start

Start with the flow demo and its guided tour. It covers the whole design in eight steps. The catalog explorer goes deeper on one layer: who owns what and why. The data store shows that same layer as the PostgreSQL schema that would hold it.

What these are not

Nothing here calls a real vendor. The browser demos model the data flow and boundaries of the design, not its runtime; the data store runs the real schema, but on a throwaway database with a made-up organisation. The explanation covers what the real systems add.
